# cook-ai-luxury

Design a premium, Persian-language website called "CookAI" (کوک‌اِی‌آی) — an intelligent AI-powered recipe generator for Persian users.

🌑 Theme:
Use an elegant, dark-mode design with luxurious aesthetics. Combine deep charcoal backgrounds (#111111), golden highlights (#E4B73B), and warm accent tones like soft orange (#FFB26B). The design should feel modern, clean, and sophisticated — like a fusion of ChatGPT and HelloFresh with a luxury twist.

---

🧠 Concept:
CookAI lets users simply type or say what they want to cook, and instantly the AI generates:
- Full ingredient list (in Persian)
- Step-by-step cooking instructions
- Estimated time and difficulty
- Nutritional information (calories, protein, carbs)
- AI-generated image of the dish
- Optional “Buy ingredients” button linking to online grocery stores

Users interact naturally — they can type “قرمه سبزی می‌خوام درست کنم” or speak it via microphone. The AI instantly returns the recipe visually in Persian.

---

📱 Target audience:
Modern Persian-speaking users (Iranian and Middle Eastern), especially food lovers, students, and working professionals who want fast, smart, and creative recipe ideas.

---

🎨 Visual Style & UI:
- Dark luxury interface with smooth glassmorphism and soft shadows.
- Elegant Persian typography with large titles and friendly tone.
- Rounded corners, motion transitions, and glowing buttons.
- Subtle background animations (floating herbs, steam, or kitchen icons).
- Fully responsive layout (mobile-first).

---

📄 Landing Page Structure:

**1️⃣ Header**
- Logo: “CookAI” in gold, modern Persian font.
- Navigation: خانه | محبوب‌ترین دستورها | درباره ما | ورود / ثبت‌نام
- “شروع آشپزی” button (highlighted gold)

**2️⃣ Hero Section**
- Big headline (in Persian): «فقط بگو چی می‌خوای بپزی!»
- Subheadline: «هوش مصنوعی در لحظه دستور پخت، مواد لازم و مراحل دقیق آشپزی رو برات آماده می‌کنه.»
- Central input box: [ بنویس یا بگو... مثلاً: قرمه سبزی می‌خوام درست کنم ]
- Microphone icon 🎙️ for voice input
- Button: «دستور پخت منو بساز»
- Background visual: dark kitchen texture + glowing AI circuit lines blended with food images.

**3️⃣ AI Result Preview Section**
- Example of AI-generated recipe:
  - Large food image (AI-generated)
  - Ingredients list in a golden-bordered box
  - Numbered steps for instructions
  - Nutritional data displayed with small icons (🔥 Calories, 💪 Protein, 🍚 Carbs)
  - Buttons: «ذخیره دستور» | «خرید مواد اولیه»

**4️⃣ Smart Suggestions**
- Title: «الهام از هوش آشپز»
- Horizontal scroll of cards showing smart AI suggestions:
  - غذای سریع برای دو نفر
  - خوراک رژیمی ایرانی
  - غذای مخصوص مهمان
  - دسر ساده با میوه

**5️⃣ Testimonials / About**
- Short section introducing CookAI:
  “با CookAI دیگه لازم نیست فکر کنی چی بپزی — فقط بگو، بقیه‌ش با ماست.”
- Optional mini testimonials from users.

**6️⃣ Footer**
- Links: درباره ما | تماس | حریم خصوصی | © 2025 CookAI
- Social icons (Instagram, Telegram, YouTube)
- Subtle golden gradient line on top of footer.
